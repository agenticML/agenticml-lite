## Why agenticml-lite

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Local-first" icon="laptop">
		Metrics, config, code, and media are written to plain files under `~/.agenticml`. Nothing leaves your machine unless you sync.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Zero-code tracking" icon="rocket">
		`agenticml run train.py` captures args, code, PyTorch components, and TensorBoard logs without editing your script.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Built-in dashboard" icon="seti:html">
		`agenticml ui` serves charts, media, and per-run notes on localhost, straight from the files.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Fast and small" icon="random">
		About 3 µs per `log()` call and about 8 bytes per scalar point on disk.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
